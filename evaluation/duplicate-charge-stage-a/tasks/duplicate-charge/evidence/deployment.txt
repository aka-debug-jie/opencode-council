The queue is at-least-once. A delivery is retried when a worker exits, returns retry, or its acknowledgement is lost.

The checkout API may receive client retries with a new request_id, but it returns the existing payment_intent_id for the same checkout operation.

During a rolling deployment, protocol-v1 and protocol-v2 payment workers consume the same queue. A redelivery can therefore move between versions. Protocol-v1 workers are expected to remain for up to 20 minutes.

The local delivery ledger retains completed rows for 30 seconds. It is an operational optimization, not the payment provider's idempotency store. The payment provider retains each caller-supplied idempotency key for seven days and guarantees one charge per key. It does not know that two different keys represent the same payment intent.

Provider timeouts are ambiguous: the charge may have been created even when the worker receives no response. A definitive ProviderUnavailable error in this fixture occurs before the provider accepts or creates a charge.
